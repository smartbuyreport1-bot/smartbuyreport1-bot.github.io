About Smart Buy Report

Smart Buy Report is an independent deals site for everyday tech: chargers, power banks, cables, hubs and audio.

How we pick deals

We list products from established brands and take the price, product name and photo from the retailer's official product data. Every deal shows the date we checked the price. Prices and stock change quickly, so always confirm the final price on the retailer's site before you buy.

What we don't do

We don't invent discounts, we don't publish coupon codes we're not allowed to share, and we don't alter product photos. If a price can't be verified, the deal doesn't go up.
